How it works

Three simple parts: play the game, earn coins, cash out. That is all there is to it.

1

Play the game

Match candies. Win levels. Climb higher and higher.

  1. Swap two candies that sit next to each other.
  2. Line up 3 or more of the same candy and they pop.
  3. Reach the level goal before your moves run out — you win the level.
  4. There are more than 10,000 levels, so the fun does not run out.
  5. Levels get harder from level 5. Plan your moves.
  6. Stuck on a hard level? Watch a short ad to get extra moves, a free booster, or an extra life.

🔨 Hammer — 100 coins

Smashes any one candy out of your way.

🔀 Shuffle — 200 coins

Shuffles the whole board into a new mix.

✨ Magic — 300 coins

A big boost for when a level has you stuck.

❤️ Extra Life — 200 coins

One more life, so you can keep playing.

2

Earn coins

Every win puts coins in your pocket. Here is every way to earn.

  • Win a level — you get +50 coins. Win a bonus level and you get +100 coins.
  • Come back every day — claim your daily +20 coins. Log in day after day and your Score multiplier grows, up to x3.
  • Daily Challenge — tap Join. A big 24-hour countdown starts. Finish in the top 7 before time runs out, then tap Claim to collect your prize.
  • Invite your friends — when a friend you invited finishes level 10, you get +50 coins.
3

Cash out your coins

Coins turn into USDT — real money, sent to your own wallet.

20,000 coins=$1 USDT
  1. The smallest cash out is $1 — that is 20,000 coins.
  2. You can take out up to $0.20 per day.
  3. Money is sent as USDT on the BSC (BEP20) network, to your wallet address.
  4. First, one small test: when you reach level 15, you can take out $0.10, one time only. This proves your wallet works before you cash out bigger amounts. Test withdrawals pay by themselves once the pool is funded.
  5. Bigger cash outs are checked and sent by the admin for now.
  6. Your account email must be verified before you can cash out.
